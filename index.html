UDP — Connectionless Delivery
Sender → Receiver (No ACK)

UDP sends datagrams directly to the destination without connection setup. The 8-byte header is minimal for speed.

UDP Header Fields 8 Bytes (Fixed)
⚡
Why UDP for Streaming?

Video/audio streaming tolerates small packet losses but needs low latency. A slightly garbled frame is better than freezing while TCP retransmits. UDP's 8-byte header vs TCP's 20-byte header reduces overhead significantly.
